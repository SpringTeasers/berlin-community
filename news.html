News & Events

One page, two questions: what is going on in Berlin right now, and what does the town do every year? Both answers change — so this page tells you what to look for and who to follow for dates.

This page is a guide, not a bulletin board

News, meetings and event dates change constantly, and this site is not an official town channel. Always confirm dates, times and cancellations on the official town website or with the event’s own organizers before you plan around them.

News

Berlin’s day-to-day news is covered by local and regional outlets, and the town publishes its own notices and meeting information. Because news moves faster than a static site can, this page points you to the reporting rather than restating it.

Events

Annual

Berlin Fair

The annual Berlin Fair is the town’s signature gathering — rides, games, and local vendors, and the one stretch of the year when the whole community turns out in the same place.

It is a recurring tradition rather than a fixed date on this page: check the fair’s own schedule or the town’s announcements for this year’s dates.

Recurring

Community Outreach Event

A town community outreach event focused on local programs and services, built to connect residents with what is available to them and to strengthen community engagement.

Confirm the current schedule with the town.

Summer – fall

Berlin Farmers Market

Running weekly through the summer and early fall, the farmers market brings together local produce and crafts — part shopping, part social hour, entirely seasonal.

Market days and hours shift with the season; confirm them with the organizers.

About these listings

These are the sources’ recurring events. No specific dates, times, or ticket prices are listed here because the research did not confirm any — and an out-of-date date is worse than no date. See Things to Do: Seasonal Events for the same traditions described by place rather than calendar.
