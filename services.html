Departments

Police Department

Berlin’s police department handles public safety, permits, and community programs. For emergencies always call 911; for everything else, start on the town’s police page.

Fire Department

Fire and emergency services for the town, with prevention and safety information published alongside their contact details. For emergencies always call 911.

Public Library

Berlin-Peck Memorial Library

The town’s public library — books, programs, and community space. It is the quickest route to local research, events, and resources for families. The library keeps its own site with current hours and events.

Schools

Berlin High School

Public schooling for the town is served by Berlin High School and the wider district. Enrollment, calendars, and every school’s own page live on the district’s site.

Berlin High School on the town website
