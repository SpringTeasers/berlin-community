Things to Do in Berlin

Berlin trades big attractions for everyday ones: wooded trails, a public golf course, a museum that keeps the town’s story, and a fair that brings the whole community out once a year.

Parks Trails Landmarks Events

Parks & Green Space

Photo to follow: wide green-space view at Timberlin Park.

Timberlin Park

The town’s anchor for outdoor recreation. Timberlin Park packs several reasons to visit into one place: hiking trails, a golf course, and picnic areas for an unhurried afternoon. It is the easiest single stop to recommend to someone who wants a few hours outside without driving far.

Trails & the Rail Trail

Photo to follow: trail through woods on the Mattabesett.

Long-distance trail

Mattabesett Trail

For hikers who want more than a loop, the Mattabesett Trail runs through Berlin as part of the larger Metacomet Ridge — a long-distance route that connects the town’s woods to a much bigger system of ridgeline trails across the region. Come with proper shoes and time to spare.

Landmarks & Historic Places

Berlin Historical Society Museum

The place to go when you want the town’s backstory told properly. The museum focuses on local history and offers guided tours, making it a natural companion to the About Berlin page — read the history, then see the objects.

Read the town’s story first

Berlin Fair

An annual tradition built around rides, games, and local vendors, the Berlin Fair is the event that gathers the whole town in one place. It is best described as Berlin’s calendar event rather than a date on this site.

Seasonal Events

Summer – fall

Berlin Farmers Market

Open weekly during the summer and early fall, the farmers market brings together local produce and crafts. It is the seasonal stop for anyone who wants to eat, shop, and meet the people who grow and make things nearby.

Market days and hours shift with the season — confirm current details with the organizers before you go.
