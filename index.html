Welcome to Berlin, Connecticut

A plain-language guide to the town at the geographic center of Connecticut — where to go, who to call, and where to confirm the details that matter.

Contact the town Explore things to do

Berlin is a working Connecticut town that measures itself by its center. Incorporated in 1785, it built its early identity on agriculture and industry — the tin trade and brick manufacturing — and it still keeps residential neighborhoods and employers side by side. This site gathers what a resident or a visitor needs into one place, and points to the official town website for everything that changes.
